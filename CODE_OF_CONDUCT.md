# Code of Conduct (Contributor Covenant 2.1, abridged)

> En español: [`CODE_OF_CONDUCT.es.md`](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.es.md).

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Rino Docs a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, background, identity, or experience level. This is a Beta-stage
educational project; be patient with newcomers.

## Expected behavior

- Use welcoming and inclusive language (project language for UI/issues is Spanish or English).
- Respect differing viewpoints; focus on technical facts and evidence.
- Accept constructive criticism; keep threads on-topic, one issue per thread.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, doxxing, or publishing private data (including student data from `.docx`/`.pdf` files).
- Spam, promo, or political/ideological proselytizing in issues, PRs, or PDF outputs.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, close, or remove contributions that violate this code and may
temporarily or permanently ban repeat offenders. Reports: open a private security
advisory on GitHub or contact the maintainer listed in `package.json` (`author` field).
All reports are handled confidentially.
